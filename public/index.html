      FAQ

      Questions, answered.

      How quickly do I get my rank?

      Most perks unlock within 60 seconds. If you're online during checkout you'll see a chat broadcast — otherwise everything is waiting at /perks on your next login.

      Is this pay-to-win?

      No. We only sell cosmetics, conveniences (extra homes, vaults, fly in lobby) and supporter cosmetics. PvP gear and stats are identical for every player.

      Can I gift a rank to a friend?

      Yes — at checkout, enter their Minecraft username instead of yours and add a note. They'll get a chat message thanking you when they log in.

      What payment methods do you accept?

      Visa, Mastercard, AmEx, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and major cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, USDC). All processed by PCI-compliant providers — we never see your card details.

      What's your refund policy?

      Donations are non-refundable in principle (they support the server), but we'll always refund duplicate charges, accidental purchases reported within 7 days, and missing perks that we can't deliver.

      Do you ever ask for my Minecraft password?

      Never. We only need your in-game username to attach perks to your account. Anyone asking for your password is a scammer — please report them in #report on Discord.

      Ready to join the realm?

      Add play.emeraldcraft.gg to your Minecraft client and jump in. Pick a rank when you're ready — there's no pressure.
